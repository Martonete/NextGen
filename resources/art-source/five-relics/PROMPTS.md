# Prompts de arte

Herramienta integrada imagegen; un llamado por pieza. PNG RGBA originales conservados en esta carpeta. El atlas de juego se adapta con el importador offline.

## solar

Use case: stylized-concept. Production Argentum Online classic 2D RPG PIXEL ART animation sprite sheet. HEADLESS equippable armor: ivory steel and warm antique gold plate, compact sun-shaped pauldrons, deep burgundy waist cloth, NO WINGS. Noble solar knight. EXACTLY 16 sprites in a precisely aligned 4 columns by 4 rows equal-cell grid, square canvas. Row1 NORTH rear view, Row2 EAST right profile, Row3 SOUTH front view, Row4 WEST left profile. Each row shows 4 walking poses: feet together, left foot forward, feet together, right foot forward. Arms counter swing; same neck opening position and feet baseline in every cell. North row shows ONLY BACK plate or back cloth with NO chest ornament; south shows front. NO HEAD FACE OR HELMET; empty neck opening for engine-rendered head. Compact AO proportions, torso/legs same scale in all directions. No weapon or shield. True transparent background alpha zero outside sprites. DO NOT DRAW CHECKERBOARD or black backdrop. No shadow, no labels, no grid lines, no text. Every sprite entirely inside its cell with 12% margin, neck opening at horizontal center 27% height, soles at 85% height. Dark pixel outlines, crisp clustered limited palette shading, designed to read at about 42 pixels tall in game. No baked aura, no glow clouds. Consistent design and scale, no overlapping cells.
